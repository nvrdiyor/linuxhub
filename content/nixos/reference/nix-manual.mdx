## Asosiy bo'limlar

- **Installation** — Nix'ni o'rnatish.
- **Package Management** — `nix-env`, `nix-shell`, `nix-build`.
- **Writing Nix Expressions** — Nix tili.
- **Advanced Topics** — distributed builds, remote stores.
- **Command Reference** — `nix`, `nix-store`, `nix-instantiate`.
- **Builtins** — barcha `builtins.*` funksiyalari.
