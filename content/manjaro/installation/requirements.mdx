Manjaro Arch asosida bo'lgani sababli apparat talablari moderat. Desktop muhitiga qarab farqlanadi.[¹](https://wiki.manjaro.org/index.php/Installation_Guides)

<Callout type="info" title="Bu darsda nimani o'rganasiz">
- Manjaro uchun minimal va tavsiya etilgan resurslar.
- Desktop bo'yicha farq (XFCE, GNOME, KDE).
- Arxitekturalar.
</Callout>

## 1. Minimal va tavsiya etilgan

| Resurs | Minimal | Tavsiya |
|---|---|---|
| Protsessor | 1 GHz | 2+ yadroli, 2 GHz+ |
| RAM | 2 GB | 4-8 GB |
| Disk | 30 GB | 40+ GB SSD |
| Grafika | 1024×768 | 1080p+ |
| Internet | O'rnatish + AUR/yangilanish | — |

## 2. Desktop bo'yicha farq

| Desktop | RAM minimal | Tavsiya | Mos |
|---|---|---|---|
| **XFCE** | 1 GB | 2-4 GB | Eski apparat, batareya |
| **GNOME** | 4 GB | 8 GB | Modern, sodda |
| **KDE Plasma** | 2 GB | 4-8 GB | Moslashtirish |
| **i3** (community) | 512 MB | 1 GB | Minimal, klaviatura |

## 3. Arxitekturalar

- **amd64 (x86_64)** — eng keng tarqalgan.
- **arm64** — Manjaro ARM Raspberry Pi va boshqa qurilmalar uchun (alohida loyiha).

## 4. Virtual mashina vs fizik

| | VM | Fizik |
|---|---|---|
| Sinab ko'rish | ✅ | Tavsiya etilmaydi |
| Performance | ~80% | 100% |
| Snapshot | ✅ | Faqat fayl tizimi (btrfs/zfs) |
| Apparat ulanish | Cheklangan | To'liq |

Boshlovchilar uchun VM bilan boshlash tavsiya etiladi.

## 5. BIOS/UEFI

Manjaro UEFI va Legacy BIOS — ikkalasini ham qo'llab-quvvatlaydi. Modern kompyuterlar uchun UEFI default.

Secure Boot — Manjaro 24+ qo'llab-quvvatlaydi, lekin ba'zi konfiguratsiyalarda o'chirish kerak bo'lishi mumkin.

## 6. Mashqlar

1. O'z kompyuteringizning RAM va CPU yadrolari sonini bilib oling. Manjaro qaysi desktop bilan eng mos?
2. [manjaro.org/download](https://manjaro.org/download/) — XFCE, GNOME, KDE ISO hajmlarini solishtiring.

## 7. Bilim sinovi

- b) 1 GB

- d) 4 GB
